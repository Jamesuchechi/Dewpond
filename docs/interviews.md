# Dewpond — Week 1 User & Compliance Validation Guide

> **Goal:** Validate demand, trust channel, and the single v1 headline wedge (**Diff** vs. **Redact**) *before* writing Milestone M0 application code.
> **Rule:** Ask about the *last real file and real hour wasted*, never "Would you use an app that...?"

---

## Target Cohort (13 short calls, 15 minutes each)

- **10 practitioners:** 5 constrained data/compliance analysts + 5 finance/ops/reconciliation staff who touch bank exports, ERP CSVs, or messy Excel workbooks weekly.
- **3 IT / InfoSec / compliance gatekeepers:** people who actually enforce DLP, browser policies, or vendor-tool approval at target companies.

---

## Script A — Practitioners (10 calls, 15 min)

### 1. Ground in a real recent pain (4 min)
1. *"Walk me through the last messy CSV or Excel file that made you lose an hour (or broke a downstream import)."*
   - Listen for: where the file came from (bank portal, ERP, CRM, vendor), approximate size/row count, file format (`.csv` vs `.xlsx`).
2. *"What broke when you opened it?"*
   - Listen without prompting for: stripped leading zeros (`080...`), scientific notation (`1.23E+10`), flipped `dd/mm` vs `mm/dd`, merged header rows / totals rows, encoding (`£`/`₦`), or Excel freezing on size.
3. *"What did you actually end up doing to fix it?"*
   - Watch what they trust today: Power Query? Python? Manual Excel formatting? Risking a free web converter? Asking an engineer?

### 2. Force-rank the headline workflow (5 min)
Pick up on whichever pain came up, then force a trade-off across these three jobs:
4. *"Across these three pains in the last 30 days, which one actually cost you the most time or stress — and what did you use for it?"*
   - **(A) Unmess:** Fixing broken IDs/dates/headers/encoding from exports without Excel corrupting them.
   - **(B) Diff / Compare:** Figuring out what rows/cells changed between two monthly exports or ledger vs. bank statement.
   - **(C) Redact:** Scrubbing customer/employee PII (phones, BVNs/NINs, emails, cards, accounts) locally before sending a file to a vendor, auditor, or AI tool.
5. *"If a tool solved ONLY your #1 choice cleanly and did nothing else, would you switch from your current workaround? Why or why not?"*

### 3. Reproducibility & SQL appetite (3 min)
6. *"When the next month's export arrives from the same system, do you redo the cleanup manually or reuse a script/Power Query?"*
7. *"Would seeing the exact SQL generated by your clicks help you (or your team/auditor), or feel intimidating/noisy?"*

### 4. Browser trust & delivery format (3 min)
8. *"Imagine you need to clean or redact a sensitive payroll or customer file right now. Would you be allowed — and personally comfortable — opening a static GitHub Pages / Netlify web app in Chrome, or would you only run something installed locally on your laptop?"*
9. *"If you had to prove to your security lead that the tool sent zero bytes over the network, what would convince them? (DevTools Network tab? Open-source repo? Company-hosted URL? IT-approved desktop binary?)"*

---

## Script B — IT / InfoSec / Compliance Gatekeepers (3 calls, 15 min)

1. *"What happens today when an analyst or ops person needs to convert, dedupe, diff, or strip PII from a sensitive CSV/Excel file?"*
2. *"Do your browser/DLP policies allow employees to open unapproved static web tools (e.g., `dewpond.app` or `*.github.io`) for sensitive internal CSVs if the app runs 100% client-side with CSP `connect-src 'self'`?"*
   - Follow-up: *"What is the exact blocker if 'no'?"* (e.g., domain allowlists, fear of supply-chain/CDN tampering, preference for self-hosting inside company intranet or a signed offline desktop app.)
3. *"Between (a) a public URL with reproducible build hashes + 60-second DevTools audit, (b) a single self-hosted static zip on your intranet, and (c) a signed offline desktop app (Tauri/Electron), which one gets approved in days instead of months?"*

---

## Decision Gate (End of Week 1)

| Signal Observed | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥ 6/10 practitioners rank **Redact** #1 or #2 with an urgent recent story (sharing with vendors/auditors/LLMs) | Lock **Unmess + Redact** as the v1 headline; keep Diff basic or post-v1. |
| ≥ 6/10 practitioners rank **Diff** #1 with recurring monthly reconciliation pain | Lock **Unmess + Diff** as the v1 headline; move Redact to v1.1. |
| ≥ 7/10 ops people say *"all our pain is 40–100 MB `.xlsx` workbooks with formulas, never `.csv`"* AND hate converting to CSV | **Warning:** Browser WASM XLSX hits hard memory/speed limits past ~25 MB (see [`docs/spikes/00-falsification-report.md`](./spikes/00-falsification-report.md)). Re-evaluate native desktop/DuckDB-CLI wrapper (`duckdb -ui` / Tauri) instead of browser-only. |
| ≥ 2/3 InfoSec leads ban unapproved public web URLs regardless of CSP | Keep static web core, add **self-hosted intranet zip + signed offline Tauri shell** as primary distribution for compliance persona. |
| Practitioners already solve all 3 smoothly in Power Query / Python / OpenRefine and won't switch | **Kill or pivot** before writing M0. |
